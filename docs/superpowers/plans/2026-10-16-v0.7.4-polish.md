## Review Focus

1. Toast with `focusAction` that becomes visible later (4th in queue, or after `hold`) while the user is focused on a page control → focus must stay on that control. (Task 2 test.)
2. Focused toast disappears while another toast is visible → focus goes to that toast's action, not `main`. (Task 2 test.)
3. Two cards + Hero of the same series mounted, Hero's live refetch lands → all show the new info, and only one `anilist.forTitle` call happened. (Task 3 test.)
4. Live refetch returns the same past `airingAt` repeatedly → retries stop after 24 h past airing, no tight loop. (Task 4 test.)
5. Undo of a mark after leaving the detail when the series was removed meanwhile → no throw, no `update` call. (Task 5 test.)
