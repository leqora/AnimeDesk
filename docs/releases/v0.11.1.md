## Srpski

### Ispravke
- Kad pretraga nađe tačno jednu seriju (česte su to pretrage sa kartica „Propušteni nastavci”), stranica sa epizodama više nije bez naslova: AnimeDesk za nekoliko sekundi sam sazna naslov serije i prikaže ga, a do tada piše „Tražim naslov…”.
- „Dodaj u watchlist” i „Preuzmi izabrane” u tom slučaju više ne dodaju seriju bez naslova — uključe se tek kad je naslov poznat. „Gledaj” radi odmah.
- Kvalitet i sub/dub sačuvani za tu seriju sada se primenjuju i kad je ona jedini rezultat pretrage.

## English

### Fixes
- When a search finds exactly one series (common for searches from the "Missed sequels" cards), the episode page no longer has an empty title: AnimeDesk finds the series title on its own within a few seconds and shows it, with "Finding the title…" until then.
- In that case "Add to watchlist" and "Download selected" no longer add a series without a title — they turn on once the title is known. "Watch" works right away.
- The quality and sub/dub saved for that series now apply when it is the only search result, too.
