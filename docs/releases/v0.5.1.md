## Srpski

### Ispravke
- **Ikone u plejeru:** dugmad za pauzu, premotavanje, prethodnu/sledeću epizodu, zvuk i titlove ponovo imaju ikone.
- **Prečice u plejeru:** posle klika na traku vremena ili jačinu zvuka, Space, strelice i ostale prečice i dalje rade. Slova (F, M, S, N) rade i kad je tastatura na ćirilici.
- **„Završeno” tek posle svih epizoda:** serija se više ne označava kao završena samo zato što je pogledana poslednja epizoda.
- **Serije koje se još emituju:** podaci sa AniList-a (npr. broj epizoda) osvežavaju se jednom dnevno umesto da ostanu zauvek kakvi su bili prvi put.
- **Umesto praznog prozora — poruka o grešci:** ako jedan deo aplikacije naiđe na grešku, prikazuje se poruka, a ostatak aplikacije radi dalje. Ako se podešavanja ne učitaju, tu je dugme „Pokušaj ponovo”.

### Poboljšanja
- **Preuzimanje bez duplikata:** ista epizoda se ne dodaje dvaput u red, a već preuzete se preskaču. Novo dugme „Ponovi neuspele” vraća sva neuspela preuzimanja u red.
- **Brže učitavanje:** aplikacija je upola manja, a statistika profila se ne računa ponovo pri svakom prelasku između stranica.

## English

### Fixes
- **Player icons:** the pause, seek, previous/next episode, sound and subtitle buttons have their icons again.
- **Player shortcuts:** Space, the arrow keys and the other shortcuts keep working after you click the time or volume slider. The letter keys (F, M, S, N) also work with a Cyrillic keyboard layout.
- **"Completed" only after every episode:** a series is no longer marked completed just because its last episode was watched.
- **Series still airing:** AniList info (e.g. the episode count) is refreshed once a day instead of staying as it was first seen.
- **An error message instead of a blank window:** if one part of the app runs into an error, a message is shown and the rest of the app keeps working. If the settings cannot be loaded, there is a "Try again" button.

### Improvements
- **Downloads without duplicates:** the same episode is not queued twice, and episodes already downloaded are skipped. A new "Retry failed" button puts all failed downloads back in the queue.
- **Faster loading:** the app is half the size, and the profile statistics are no longer recomputed on every page change.
