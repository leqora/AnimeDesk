## Srpski

### Novo
- Nova stranica „Raspored” u meniju: epizode koje izlaze u narednih 7 dana, po danima i satu emitovanja (vreme je po tvom računaru).
- „Moje serije” prikazuje serije iz tvoje Watchlist-e, a „Sve” sve što se emituje te nedelje na AniList-u (bez sadržaja za odrasle).
- Prikazuje se samo broj epizode i vreme — nikad naslov ili opis epizode. Kasnije sezone i sporedne priče serija koje nisu u tvojoj Watchlist-i su bez postera, da ne otkriju ništa.
- Klik na seriju iz Watchlist-e otvara njen detalj, a klik na ostale pokreće pretragu.
- Raspored se preuzima samo kad otvoriš stranicu i čuva se nekoliko sati.

## English

### New
- New "Schedule" page in the menu: episodes airing in the next 7 days, by day and air time (times use your computer's time zone).
- "My series" shows series from your Watchlist, "All" shows everything airing that week on AniList (no adult content).
- Only the episode number and time are shown — never an episode title or description. Later seasons and side stories of series that are not in your Watchlist have no poster, so nothing is given away.
- Clicking a series from your Watchlist opens its page; clicking any other series searches for it.
- The schedule is fetched only when you open the page and is kept for a few hours.
