## Srpski

### Novo
- **Brzina reprodukcije:** u meniju plejera (zupčanik) biraš brzinu od 0,5× do 2×, a prečice `[` (sporije), `]` (brže) i `\` (nazad na 1×) rade po fizičkom tasteru (na srpskom rasporedu to su Š, Đ i Ž). Svaka epizoda uvek kreće od 1×.
- **Traka vremena pokazuje više:** vidi se koliko je već učitano, a ispod miša se prikazuje vreme (i deo epizode, npr. uvod ili odjava).

### Poboljšanja
- **Zupčanik je sada „Podešavanja plejera”** i uvek je dostupan; u njemu su brzina i titlovi.
- **Brže pokretanje aplikacije:** plejer se učitava tek kad zatreba (glavni deo aplikacije je oko tri puta manji, 928 kB → 316 kB), a priprema se u pozadini čim aplikacija miruje.

## English

### New
- **Playback speed:** pick a speed from 0.5× to 2× in the player menu (gear), and use the `[` (slower), `]` (faster) and `\` (back to 1×) shortcuts, which work by physical key (on the Serbian layout those are Š, Đ and Ž). Every episode always starts at 1×.
- **A more informative timeline:** it shows how much is already buffered, and the time under the mouse (plus the part of the episode, e.g. intro or ending).

### Improvements
- **The gear is now "Player settings"** and is always available; it holds speed and subtitles.
- **Faster app startup:** the player loads only when needed (the main part of the app is about three times smaller, 928 kB → 316 kB) and is prepared in the background once the app is idle.
