## Srpski

### Ispravke
- „Šta je novo” ponovo prikazuje svaku stavku u svom redu (stavke su se lepile u jedan red).
- Automatsko preuzimanje: ako je aplikacija bila ugašena dok se serija završavala, poslednje epizode sezone se sada ipak preuzmu same.
- U detalju serije prazna traka na dnu više ne prekriva poslednji red dugmića epizoda.

## English

### Fixes
- "What's new" shows each item on its own line again (items used to run together on one line).
- Automatic downloads: if the app was off while a series was finishing, the last episodes of the season are now still downloaded on their own.
- In a series' detail page, an empty bar at the bottom no longer covers the last row of episode buttons.
