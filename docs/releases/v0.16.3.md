## Srpski

### Ispravke
- Traka „Pušta se u mpv-u” je sada potpuno neprovidna: pri skrolovanju se kroz nju više ne nazire sadržaj stranice.
- Traka sa pretragom, žanrom, sortiranjem i „Dodaj ručno” na Watchlist-i staje u jedan red i više ne skače u dva reda kad se vratiš na stranicu.

## English

### Fixes
- The "Playing in mpv" bar is now fully opaque: the page no longer shows through it while scrolling.
- The Watchlist bar with search, genre, sorting and "Add manually" fits in one row and no longer jumps to two rows when you come back to the page.

### Under the hood
- Updated Electron (45). The AniList login is stored the way future Electron versions require; if you are logged in, you stay logged in.
