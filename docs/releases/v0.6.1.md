## Srpski

### Poboljšanja
- **Titlovi se prilagođavaju plejeru:** rastu i opadaju sa veličinom prozora (na punom ekranu su dovoljno veliki), a dva reda stoje zbijeno, u jednom okviru. Titl se podiže iznad kontrola dok su vidljive, a spušta kad se sakriju.
- **Podešavanja titla:** u Podešavanjima → Titlovi biraš veličinu, razmak redova, font, boju i tamni okvir (i njegovu providnost), uz živi pregled. „Vrati podrazumevano” vraća sve na početno.
- **Meni titlova u plejeru:** pored dugmeta titla je novo dugme sa prekidačem titla, pomakom titla i klizačem veličine.
- **Pomak titla:** `G` pomera titl ranije, `H` kasnije za 0,1 s (uz Shift za 1 s). Pomak se pamti po seriji i posebno za sub i dub, pa ne moraš da ga podešavaš svaku epizodu.
- **Dub epizode:** titl je tu podrazumevano isključen; uključeno/isključeno se pamti posebno za sub i dub.
- **SRT titlovi:** rade u ugrađenom plejeru, i preko strima i kao `.srt` pored preuzete epizode.
- **Jasna poruka:** ako je format titla nepodržan, plejer to javi i predloži spoljni plejer (mpv) umesto da titl tiho nestane.

## English

### Improvements
- **Subtitles adapt to the player:** they grow and shrink with the window (large enough on fullscreen), and two lines sit tightly together in a single box. Subtitles lift above the controls while those are visible and drop back when they hide.
- **Subtitle settings:** in Settings → Subtitles you pick the size, line spacing, font, colour and dark box (and its opacity), with a live preview. "Restore defaults" resets everything.
- **Subtitle menu in the player:** next to the subtitle button there is a new button with a subtitle switch, subtitle delay and a size slider.
- **Subtitle delay:** `G` moves subtitles earlier, `H` later by 0.1 s (hold Shift for 1 s). The delay is remembered per series and separately for sub and dub, so you do not have to adjust it for every episode.
- **Dub episodes:** subtitles are off by default there; on/off is remembered separately for sub and dub.
- **SRT subtitles:** they work in the built-in player, both over a stream and as a `.srt` next to a downloaded episode.
- **Clear message:** if the subtitle format is not supported, the player says so and suggests the external player (mpv) instead of silently showing nothing.
