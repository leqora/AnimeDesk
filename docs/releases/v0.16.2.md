## Srpski

### Novo
- Dok epizoda ide u spoljnom mpv plejeru, na vrhu aplikacije stoji traka „Pušta se u mpv-u” sa nazivom serije i brojem epizode, na svakoj stranici.
- Dugme „Zaustavi” na traci zatvara mpv kao da si ga sam zatvorio: mesto gde si stao se pamti, a epizoda se beleži kao odgledana samo ako si odgledao dovoljno.

### Ispravke
- Serija koju gledaš, a još nisi odgledao nijednu epizodu, više ne prikazuje „N novih” za sve epizode koje su izašle.

## English

### New
- While an episode plays in the external mpv player, a "Playing in mpv" bar with the series name and episode number stays at the top of the app, on every page.
- The "Stop" button on the bar closes mpv as if you closed it yourself: where you stopped is remembered, and the episode is marked as watched only if you watched enough of it.

### Fixes
- A series you are watching but have not finished any episode of no longer shows "N new" for every episode that has aired.
