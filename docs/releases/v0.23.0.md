## Srpski

### Novo
- Pregled kadra na traci: kad držiš miš nad trakom ugrađenog plejera, iznad nje se prikazuje kadar sa tog mesta, uz vreme.
- Za epizodu koju gledaš prvi put pregled radi samo do mesta do kog si stigao gledanjem, tako da ništa ne može da ti se otkrije unapred. Odgledane epizode imaju pregled cele trake.
- Pregled ograničava potrošnju podataka i glavni video uvek ima prednost: kadrovi se uzimaju samo kad je dovoljno unapred učitano ili je video pauziran.
- Može da se isključi u Podešavanja → Plejer („Pregled kadra na traci”).

## English

### New
- Frame preview on the seek bar: when you hover over the seek bar of the built-in player, a frame from that spot is shown above it, together with the time.
- For an episode you are watching for the first time, the preview only works up to the point you have reached by watching, so nothing can be revealed ahead of time. Episodes you have already watched get a preview of the whole bar.
- The preview limits how much data it uses and the main video always has priority: frames are fetched only when enough is loaded ahead or the video is paused.
- It can be turned off in Settings → Player ("Frame preview on the seek bar").
