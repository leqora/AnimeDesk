## Srpski

### Poboljšanja
- Brži posteri: AnimeDesk sada traži podatke o serijama sa AniList-a u grupama (do 10 naslova ili 50 serija u jednom zahtevu), umesto jedan po jedan. Posteri u Pretrazi i Watchlist-i stižu brže, a AniList-ovo ograničenje od 30 zahteva u minuti se mnogo ređe dostiže — posle nekoliko pretraga zaredom posteri više ne stoje prazni po pola minuta.
- Kad AniList ipak privremeno ograniči zahteve, podaci koji su već stigli se odmah prikazuju, a pojavi se kratko obaveštenje koliko otprilike treba da se sačeka.

## English

### Improvements
- Faster posters: AnimeDesk now asks AniList for series info in groups (up to 10 titles or 50 series per request) instead of one at a time. Posters in Search and the Watchlist arrive sooner, and AniList's limit of 30 requests a minute is reached far less often — after several searches in a row, posters no longer stay empty for half a minute.
- When AniList does limit requests for a moment, info that already arrived is shown right away, and a short notice says roughly how long to wait.
