## Srpski

### Poboljšanja
- Tačniji posteri: AnimeDesk sada pažljivije upoređuje naziv serije iz pretrage sa AniList-om — poredi i alternativne nazive, zanemaruje oznake kao „(Uncensored)” ili „(season 1+season 2)” i više ne uzima prvi rezultat naslepo. Mnoge serije koje su ranije dobijale poster druge serije sada dobijaju svoj, a neke koje su bile bez postera sada ga imaju. Kad AniList nema dovoljno sličnu seriju, poster ostaje prazan umesto da bude pogrešan.
- Kad AnimeDesk nije siguran koja je serija na AniList-u, detalj serije to i kaže („Nije sigurno koja je ovo serija na AniList-u.”) i prikazuje samo poster, godinu i žanrove. Raspored, nove epizode, nazivi i sličice epizoda, filler oznake i automatsko preskakanje uvoda koriste se tek kad je serija sigurno prepoznata ili ručno izabrana.
- Ručni izbor serije sa AniList-a („Izaberi” / „Promeni” u detalju serije) sada radi i bez prijave na AniList.
- Posle ažuriranja AnimeDesk jednom ponovo proverava ranije sačuvane pogotke, pa se ranije pogrešni posteri sami ispravljaju.

## English

### Improvements
- More accurate posters: AnimeDesk now compares the series name from search with AniList more carefully — it also checks alternative titles, ignores tags such as "(Uncensored)" or "(season 1+season 2)", and no longer blindly takes the first result. Many series that used to get another series' poster now get their own, and some that had none now have one. When AniList has no close enough series, the poster stays empty instead of being wrong.
- When AnimeDesk is not sure which AniList series it is, the series page says so ("Not sure which AniList series this is.") and shows only the poster, year and genres. The airing schedule, new episodes, episode titles and thumbnails, filler marks and automatic intro skipping are used only once the series is recognized for sure or picked by hand.
- Picking the AniList series by hand ("Choose" / "Change" on the series page) now works without signing in to AniList.
- After the update, AnimeDesk re-checks previously saved matches once, so earlier wrong posters fix themselves.
