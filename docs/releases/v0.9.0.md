## Srpski

### Novo
- Automatsko preuzimanje novih epizoda: u detalju serije uključi „Automatski preuzimaj nove epizode” i AnimeDesk sam preuzima epizode kad se pojave na izvoru.
- Prekidač je po seriji, radi dok je serija u „Gledam”, i važi samo za epizode koje izađu posle njegovog uključivanja; ranije epizode se ne preuzimaju unazad. Preuzima se i poslednja epizoda sezone.
- Aplikacija mora da radi da bi preuzimala (može biti sakrivena u tray-u), a u Podešavanjima mora biti izabran folder za preuzimanje.
- Ako izvor još nema novu epizodu, na stranici „Preuzeto” se vidi „Čeka izvor” sa vremenom sledećeg pokušaja; aplikacija sama pokušava ponovo, a dugme „Pokušaj sada” to radi odmah. Automatska preuzimanja nose oznaku „Auto”.

## English

### New
- Automatic downloads of new episodes: turn on "Download new episodes automatically" in a series' detail page and AnimeDesk downloads episodes once the source has them.
- The switch is per series, works while the series is in "Watching", and only covers episodes released after you turn it on; earlier episodes are not downloaded retroactively. The last episode of a season is picked up too.
- The app has to be running to download (it can be hidden in the tray), and a download folder must be chosen in Settings.
- If the source does not have a new episode yet, the "Downloads" page shows "Waiting for the source" with the time of the next try; the app retries on its own, and "Try now" does it immediately. Automatic downloads carry an "Auto" tag.
