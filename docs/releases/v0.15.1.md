## Srpski

### Poboljšanja
- „Raspored” se otvara na prvom danu koji ima epizode, umesto na praznom „Danas” (npr. kad tvoje serije izlaze tek u petak). Dan koji sam izabereš ostaje izabran.
- „Pokušaj ponovo” sada pokazuje šta se dešava: „Osvežava se…” dok se raspored preuzima, odnosno koliko sekundi treba da sačekaš pre sledećeg pokušaja.
- Posteri u rasporedu više ne ostaju zauvek u učitavanju kad je server sa slikama spor — posle nekoliko sekundi prikaže se prazan okvir.
- Dok se aplikacija priprema pri pokretanju, redovi rasporeda su čitljivi (prigušen je samo poster).
- Serije koje izlaze u isto vreme uvek su poređane istim redom.

## English

### Improvements
- "Schedule" now opens on the first day that has episodes instead of an empty "Today" (for example when your series only air on Friday). A day you pick yourself stays selected.
- "Try again" now shows what is happening: "Refreshing…" while the schedule is loading, or how many seconds to wait before the next try.
- Schedule posters no longer stay loading forever when the image server is slow — after a few seconds an empty frame is shown.
- While the app is getting ready at start, schedule rows stay readable (only the poster is dimmed).
- Series airing at the same time are always listed in the same order.
