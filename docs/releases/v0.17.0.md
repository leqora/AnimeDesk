## Srpski

### Novo
- Nova stranica „Otkrij” u meniju: šta je popularno ove sezone, šta stiže sledeće sezone, popularni filmovi i šta je popularno sada.
- Filter žanra važi za sve šine odjednom.
- „Iznenadi me” bira nasumičnu seriju koja nije u tvojoj Watchlist-i i odmah je traži.
- Klik na seriju iz tvoje Watchlist-e otvara njen detalj, a klik na ostale pokreće pretragu. Za serije koje još nisu počele prikazuje se samo kad počinju.
- Prikazuju se samo naslov, poster, format, godina, broj epizoda i žanrovi — nikad opis. Kasnije sezone i sporedne priče se ne prikazuju (osim ako je serija već u tvojoj Watchlist-i), a sadržaja za odrasle nema.
- Podaci se preuzimaju samo kad otvoriš stranicu i čuvaju se 12 sati.

### Ispravke
- Ako sistem za šifrovanje Windows-a zastane pri pokretanju, AnimeDesk više ne čeka beskonačno na učitavanje AniList prijave: posle najviše 5 sekundi nastavlja, a prijava se učita čim bude spremna.

## English

### New
- New "Discover" page in the menu: what is popular this season, what is coming next season, popular movies and what is trending right now.
- The genre filter applies to all rows at once.
- "Surprise me" picks a random series that is not in your Watchlist and searches for it right away.
- Clicking a series from your Watchlist opens its page; clicking any other series searches for it. Series that have not started yet only show their start date.
- Only the title, poster, format, year, episode count and genres are shown — never a description. Later seasons and side stories are not shown (unless the series is already in your Watchlist), and there is no adult content.
- The data is fetched only when you open the page and is kept for 12 hours.

### Fixes
- If Windows encryption stalls at startup, AnimeDesk no longer waits forever for the AniList login to load: it continues after at most 5 seconds and the login loads as soon as it is ready.
