## Srpski

### Poboljšanja
- Brže pokretanje: ako je poslednja provera izvora (ani-cli) bila uspešna u poslednja 3 dana i ani-cli se od tada nije menjao, semafor je odmah zelen i „Nastavi”, ▶ i Pretraga rade odmah, umesto posle ~10 sekundi. Provera se i dalje radi, samo u pozadini; ako izvor ne radi, AnimeDesk kao i do sada pokuša da ažurira ani-cli (posle završetka gledanja i preuzimanja), a ako ni to ne pomogne, semafor pocrveni.
- Automatsko ažuriranje ani-cli-ja i yt-dlp-a čeka da se gledanje i preuzimanja završe, da ne bi menjalo alate dok se koriste.

## English

### Improvements
- Faster start: if the last source check (ani-cli) succeeded within the last 3 days and ani-cli has not changed since, the status light is green right away and "Continue", ▶ and Search work immediately instead of after ~10 seconds. The check still runs, in the background; if the source is down, AnimeDesk tries to update ani-cli as before (once watching and downloads are finished), and if that does not help, the light turns red.
- Automatic updates of ani-cli and yt-dlp now wait until watching and downloads are finished, so the tools are never replaced while in use.
