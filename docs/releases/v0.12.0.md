## Srpski

### Novo
- Watchlist ima pretragu: kucaj deo naslova (radi i bez kvačica i velikih slova, i po japanskom naslovu). `/` ili Ctrl+F skače u polje, Esc ga briše.
- Statusi su sada dugmići sa brojem serija („Sve 42 · Gledam 5 · …”), a tu su i filter po žanru i sortiranje „Poslednje dodato”. Kad ništa ne odgovara filterima, „Poništi filtere” sve vraća.
- Podešavanja → „Podaci”: izvoz rezervne kopije u jedan fajl (lista, dnevnik gledanja, pozicije, postavke po seriji i podešavanja) i uvoz uz pregled pre zamene. Pre svakog uvoza AnimeDesk sam sačuva kopiju trenutnih podataka.
- Automatska dnevna rezervna kopija u folderu podataka (čuva se poslednjih 7).
- „Otvori folder podataka” i „Obriši keš slika i opisa” (uz prikaz veličine keša).
- AniList prijava se nikad ne upisuje u rezervnu kopiju.

## English

### New
- The watchlist can be searched: type part of a title (works without accents or capitals, and by the Japanese title too). `/` or Ctrl+F jumps to the field, Esc clears it.
- Statuses are now buttons with series counts ("All 42 · Watching 5 · …"), plus a genre filter and a "Recently added" sort. When nothing matches, "Reset filters" brings everything back.
- Settings → "Data": export a backup to a single file (list, watch log, positions, per-series settings and settings) and import it with a preview before anything is replaced. Before every import AnimeDesk saves a copy of the current data on its own.
- Automatic daily backup in the data folder (the last 7 are kept).
- "Open data folder" and "Clear image and description cache" (with the cache size shown).
- Your AniList login is never written into a backup.
