## Srpski

### Ispravke
- **Slike se više ne gube u velikim pretragama:** AniList dozvoljava ograničen broj zahteva u minuti, a pretraga sa mnogo rezultata ih je trošila odjednom. Aplikacija sada šalje zahteve jedan po jedan i sačeka kad AniList zatraži pauzu, pa se slike pojavljuju postepeno umesto da ostanu prazne.
- **Podnaslov posle crte:** naslovi poput „… Movie 4 - The Lost Tower” se sada traže i po delu posle crte.
- Naslovi za koje AniList nema poklapanje pamte se 7 dana, da ne troše zahteve pri svakoj pretrazi.

## English

### Fixes
- **Posters no longer go missing in large searches:** AniList allows a limited number of requests per minute, and a search with many results used them all at once. The app now sends requests one at a time and waits when AniList asks it to, so posters appear gradually instead of staying empty.
- **Subtitle after a dash:** titles like "… Movie 4 - The Lost Tower" are now also looked up by the part after the dash.
- Titles AniList has no match for are remembered for 7 days, so they do not use up requests on every search.
