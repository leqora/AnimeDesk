## Srpski

### Novo
- **Nove kartice serija** na Početnoj i u Watchlist-i: napredak je sada na samom posteru — bedž „3/12” (završene serije „✓ 12/12”), tanka traka napretka na vrhu, tačka u boji statusa (gledam, završeno, planiram, pauzirano, odustao) i ocena „★ 8” u Watchlist-i. Ispod naslova piše šta je sledeće: „EP 4 · ostalo 16 min”, „Sledeća: EP 4” ili „✓ Završeno”.
- **Započeta epizoda se vidi svuda:** ako si epizodu prekinuo na pola, njen broj na listi epizoda (Brojevi) dobija traku, u prikazu Lista traka je preko sličice uz „ostalo 16 min”, a na Početnoj dugme glasi „Nastavi EP 4 od 8:15”.
- **Dugme ▶ i u Watchlist-i:** nastavak gledanja direktno sa kartice, bez otvaranja serije.

### Poboljšanja
- Kartice se pri prelasku mišem podignu i zasvetle, a dugmad ▶ i ★ se pojave; isto važi i kad do kartice dođeš tasterom Tab.
- Poster se učitava sa blagim prelazom umesto naglog pojavljivanja; dok se čeka, vidi se prazan okvir.
- Posle gledanja kartice i Početna same osveže napredak (u roku od nekoliko sekundi).
- Uz isključene animacije (Podešavanja ili sistem) kartice se ne pomeraju i ne uvećavaju.

## English

### New
- **New series cards** on Home and in the Watchlist: progress now sits on the poster itself — a "3/12" badge ("✓ 12/12" for finished series), a thin progress bar at the top, a status-colored dot (watching, completed, planned, paused, dropped) and a "★ 8" score in the Watchlist. Under the title you see what comes next: "EP 4 · 16 min left", "Next: EP 4" or "✓ Completed".
- **A started episode shows everywhere:** if you stopped an episode halfway, its number in the episode list (Numbers) gets a bar, the List view shows a bar over the thumbnail with "16 min left", and the Home button reads "Continue EP 4 from 8:15".
- **▶ button in the Watchlist too:** continue watching straight from the card without opening the series.

### Improvements
- Cards lift and glow on mouse hover and the ▶ and ★ buttons appear; the same happens when you reach a card with the Tab key.
- Posters fade in instead of popping in; while they load you see an empty frame.
- After watching, cards and Home refresh their progress by themselves (within a few seconds).
- With animations turned off (Settings or system) cards do not move or zoom.
