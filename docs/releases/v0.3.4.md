## Srpski

### Ispravke
- **Naslovi bez slike se ponovo proveravaju posle ažuriranja:** aplikacija 7 dana pamti naslove za koje AniList nema sliku, da ne bi stalno slala iste zahteve. Do sada je to pamćenje važilo i kad nova verzija donese bolju pretragu, pa su neki naslovi ostajali bez slike i do nedelju dana. Sada se, posle ažuriranja koje menja pretragu, takvi naslovi odmah traže ponovo.

## English

### Fixes
- **Titles without a poster are rechecked after an update:** the app remembers for 7 days which titles AniList has no poster for, so it doesn't keep sending the same requests. Until now that memory also applied after an update with better search, so some titles stayed without a poster for up to a week. Now, after an update that changes the search, those titles are looked up again right away.
