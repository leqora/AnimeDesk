## Srpski

### Novo
- Brza pretraga (Ctrl+K, ili dugme „Brza pretraga” u meniju): ukucaj deo naslova i za par pritisaka otvori seriju iz svoje Watchlist-e, bilo koju stranicu ili radnju (ceo ekran, komponente). Poslednji red uvek pretražuje anime po onome što si ukucao.
- Bez ukucanog teksta prikazuje serije koje trenutno gledaš, stranice i radnje. Radi strelicama, Enter i Esc, i ništa ne preuzima sa interneta.
- Pojačanje zvuka do 300 % za tihe epizode: u ugrađenom plejeru preko zupčanika („Pojačanje zvuka”) ili u Podešavanjima → Plejer. Glasni delovi se ublažavaju da zvuk ne krči.
- Pojačanje važi i u spoljnom mpv plejeru, od sledećeg pokretanja epizode.

## English

### New
- Quick search (Ctrl+K, or the "Quick search" button in the menu): type part of a title and open a series from your Watchlist, any page or an action (full screen, components) in a couple of keystrokes. The last row always searches for anime with what you typed.
- With nothing typed it shows the series you are watching, the pages and the actions. Use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc; nothing is downloaded from the internet.
- Volume boost up to 300% for quiet episodes: in the built-in player from the gear menu ("Volume boost") or in Settings → Player. Loud parts are limited so the sound does not distort.
- The boost also applies in the external mpv player, from the next episode you start.
